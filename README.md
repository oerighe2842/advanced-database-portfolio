## Selected Case Study: NaijaMart (simulated online marketplace)

**Scenario:** NaijaMart is a simulated Nigerian e-commerce platform where customers browse products, place orders, pay online, and receive deliveries across Nigerian states.

**Nature of the system:** An online retail marketplace handling product catalogues, stock, orders, payments and delivery tracking.

**Users of the database:**
- Customers (browse and buy)
- Store/inventory staff (manage products and stock)
- Finance staff (verify payments)
- Logistics staff (update delivery status)

**Major data stored:** customers, product categories, products, orders, order items, payments, deliveries.

**Key operations supported:**
- Register customers and add products
- Place orders and update stock
- Record payments
- Track delivery status
- Produce sales reports
